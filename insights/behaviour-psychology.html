Velocity is not productivity

Use delivery history for planning without turning it into a performance target.

Separate planning from performance

Velocity describes completed estimated work within one team’s own context. A rising number alone does not demonstrate greater customer value or better quality.

Inspect a balanced picture

Discuss customer outcomes, elapsed time, unfinished work, defects and team sustainability alongside delivery history. Look for changes in estimation or scope before interpreting a trend.

Use a forecast honestly

Include Sprints with zero completed work. Explain uncertainty and assumptions; update the forecast when new evidence arrives. Never compare teams by story-point totals.
