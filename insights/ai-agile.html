What AI means for Scrum Masters

AI can reduce toil while increasing the importance of judgement, facilitation and learning.

AI

6 min read · 2026

The observation

AI can reduce toil while increasing the importance of judgement, facilitation and learning.

What to inspect

Look for the structures around the behaviour: incentives, information, decision rights, queues, dependencies and the quality of feedback. Ask what the current system rewards, what it makes difficult and where learning is delayed.

A practical experiment

Make one change small. Define a hypothesis, a short timebox and two or three observable signals. Inspect with the people doing the work and keep, adapt or discard the experiment.

Questions to carry forward

  • What are we optimising for?
  • What evidence would change our mind?
  • Which behaviour is the system currently rewarding?
  • Where does work wait?
