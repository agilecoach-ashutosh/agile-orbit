Five Dysfunctions: team reflection

A practical reflection inspired by Patrick Lencioni’s team model; use it as a conversation aid, not a diagnostic score.

Trust and productive disagreement

Can we acknowledge mistakes and ask for help? Can we challenge an idea respectfully? Invite examples of when these were easy or difficult, without asking people to name or rank colleagues.

Commitment and accountability

At the end of a decision, ask each person to explain what was agreed and what concerns remain. Make follow-up actions and the next review visible.

Collective results

Agree one shared outcome and check whether individual targets compete with it. Choose one behaviour to practise over the next two weeks, then review its effect.
