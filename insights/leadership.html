Context beats control

Make decisions easier by clarifying outcomes and boundaries.

Make the decision visible

Write the decision, who is affected, when it is needed and who has the authority to make it. Distinguish advice from approval.

Provide context

Explain the desired outcome, constraints, available evidence and acceptable risks. Ask the team what information is missing before asking for a plan.

Agree a review point

Choose when to inspect the outcome and what would trigger an earlier conversation. Let people choose the approach within the agreed boundaries.
