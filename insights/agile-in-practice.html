Why Agile transformations fail

Turn a broad transformation ambition into one testable change.

Start with the friction

Ask a team to trace one recent item from request to customer use. Mark queues, handoffs and decisions that had to wait. Describe what happened without attributing motives.

Run one experiment

Choose the largest avoidable delay. Agree a small change, an owner, a review date and an outcome measure such as elapsed time or customer feedback. Keep the experiment small enough to reverse.

Review the system

Compare what happened with the expectation. Ask what helped, what became harder, and whether a local improvement pushed work onto another team. Adapt before scaling.
