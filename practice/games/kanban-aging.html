YOUR MISSION

Which card should the team discuss first?

You are not choosing who should work harder. You are choosing which aging item deserves attention because its delivery risk is increasing.

📏 Service Level Expectation (SLE): 6 days
Items are expected to finish within 6 days once work starts. As a card approaches or exceeds 6 days, its delivery risk rises. Blocked work is an additional warning signal.
