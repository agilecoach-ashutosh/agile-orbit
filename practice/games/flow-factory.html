WHAT DID YOU NOTICE?

Smaller batches change when feedback becomes possible.

When a whole batch must finish one stage before moving, downstream stations wait and value reaches the customer later. Smaller transfers allow stages to overlap, expose problems sooner and reduce the amount of work sitting in queues.

Don’t turn this into a rule that “batch 1 is always best”. Real systems have setup costs, constraints and economics. The useful question is: what is the smallest practical batch that improves feedback and flow in your context?
