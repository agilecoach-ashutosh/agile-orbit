FLOW LEARNING PATH

Maximizing Flow

Flow is not about keeping everyone busy or starting more work. It is about helping value move through the system with less waiting, fewer bottlenecks, faster feedback and better focus.

Don’t just read about flow. Experience what improves it.
01Experience →

Visualize & limit WIP

See how limiting active work makes queues visible, exposes constraints, and creates space to finish before starting more.
02Experience →

Address bottlenecks

Watch work accumulate at a constraint and discover why improving non-bottlenecks may increase activity without improving delivery.
03Experience →

Reduce handoffs & dependencies

Experience how handoffs and dependencies create waiting, coordination overhead and blocked work—and why reducing them improves flow.
04Experience →

Shorten feedback loops

See how delivering usable value sooner creates earlier feedback, exposes wrong assumptions faster, and reduces the cost of going in the wrong direction.
05Experience →

Work in smaller batches

Compare large and small batches and see their effect on waiting, first value, risk exposure and overall flow.
06Experience →

Manage queues & aging work

Watch queues grow when work arrives faster than downstream capacity, then use aging and SLE signals to identify where waiting is becoming risky.
07Experience →

Optimize “time in the zone”

“Time in the zone” means giving teams and individuals uninterrupted, focused blocks of time for complex work without distractions. Compare that focus with frequent context switching and see how interruptions delay completion and value.
08Experience →

Remove policies that obstruct flow

See how excessive WIP, specialist-only working, unmanaged blockers and indiscriminate expedite work can obstruct flow—and what changes when those policies are challenged.

Start with one lever at a time. Then run Kanban Flow Lab → to experience what happens when WIP, bottlenecks, blockers, prioritization and flow metrics interact in the same system.
