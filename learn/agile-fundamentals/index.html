Agile Fundamentals

A practical foundation: value, feedback, adaptation and sustainable delivery.

Key takeaway: Agile is best understood as a way of working under uncertainty. The emphasis is on short feedback loops, close collaboration and the ability to adapt when evidence changes.

Concept

Agile is best understood as a way of working under uncertainty. The emphasis is on short feedback loops, close collaboration and the ability to adapt when evidence changes.

What happens in teams

Start with the problem rather than the framework. Ask what outcome matters, how quickly you can learn, where work gets stuck and what constraints shape behaviour.

Agile implication

Use frameworks as useful constraints, not as substitutes for judgement.

What you can do

  1. Make the current system visible.
  2. Ask one question that challenges an assumption.
  3. Run a small, reversible experiment.
  4. Inspect evidence and decide what to do next.

Practical example

Choose one recurring friction—handoffs, unclear priorities, excessive WIP or delayed feedback. Map the path from request to outcome, identify where waiting occurs and test one change with the people doing the work.
