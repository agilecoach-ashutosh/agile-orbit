Behaviour Change

Turn desired behaviours into small, observable experiments supported by context.

Use this as a reflection lens. This content focuses on workplace and organisational behaviour, not clinical psychological advice.

What it means

Turn desired behaviours into small, observable experiments supported by context.

What to look for

  • Observable behaviours and repeated patterns.
  • Context, incentives and constraints.
  • How people respond to uncertainty and feedback.
  • Where the system makes a desired behaviour easier or harder.
