Kanban

Make work visible and actively manage its flow.

Connect the ideas to real work

Define the workflow, make work and policies visible, manage items actively, and improve the workflow. Examine work in progress, throughput, work item age and cycle time together.

Try this with your team

Trace five recent items through your workflow. Where did they wait? Agree one explicit policy for starting work and one action for ageing items. Review whether the change helps items finish sooner.

Review what changed

Write down what you expected, what happened and what you learned. Decide together whether to keep, adapt or stop the experiment.
