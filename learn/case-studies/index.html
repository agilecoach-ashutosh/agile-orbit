HOW TO USE THE LIBRARY

Read like a decision-maker, not a spectator

Every full case uses the same learning rhythm so patterns become comparable across industries.

01 · SituationUnderstand the context and pressure before knowing the answer.
02 · TensionIdentify the system signals, constraints and competing goals.
03 · DecideChoose what you would do before the outcome is revealed.
04 · EvidenceCompare your reasoning with what happened and what remained difficult.
05 · TransferExtract a principle you can test without blindly copying the solution.
