Connect the ideas to real work

Use the framework’s current guidance to understand its roles, events and practices. Keep the practical conversation focused on customer value, coordination, quality and evidence rather than completion of a planning checklist.

Try this with your team

Select one shared objective. Identify the teams needed to achieve it, make assumptions and dependencies explicit, and agree how integrated progress will be reviewed. Surface unresolved risks before treating the plan as reliable.

Review what changed

Write down what you expected, what happened and what you learned. Decide together whether to keep, adapt or stop the experiment.
