Scrum

Connect goals, usable outcomes and inspection.

Connect the ideas to real work

Scrum brings the Product Owner, Scrum Master and Developers together around product value. The Sprint creates a regular opportunity to produce a usable Increment, inspect progress and adapt. A Sprint Goal provides focus while the plan evolves.

Try this with your team

Choose a real Sprint Goal. Ask the team to explain how today’s plan supports it, what has changed, and what collaboration would help. Inspect outcomes with stakeholders instead of treating completed activity as the result.

Review what changed

Write down what you expected, what happened and what you learned. Decide together whether to keep, adapt or stop the experiment.
