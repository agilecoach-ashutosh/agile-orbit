Privacy & Analytics

A simple explanation of what this site measures and what happens when you choose to contact me.

Website analytics

Agile Orbit uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is being used and which content is useful. The implementation records page views and lightweight interaction events such as navigation clicks, outbound links, resource downloads, selected search terms and button actions.

IP anonymisation is enabled in the site configuration. Analytics is used to improve the website and its content, not to build individual visitor profiles within Agile Orbit.

Contact

The Contact page uses direct links to email and LinkedIn. Agile Orbit does not host a contact form or store messages on the website. If you choose to email or contact me through LinkedIn, your communication is handled by the service you use for that interaction.

Third-party services

Some links take you to external websites or services. Their own privacy terms and policies apply once you leave Agile Orbit.

Questions
