Behavioural Psychology

Understand workplace behaviour through practical, non-clinical lenses.

Key takeaway: Teams are social systems. Behaviour is shaped by incentives, norms, perceived safety, identity, context and the consequences of speaking up.

Concept

Teams are social systems. Behaviour is shaped by incentives, norms, perceived safety, identity, context and the consequences of speaking up.

What happens in teams

Treat behavioural explanations as hypotheses. Observe patterns, ask questions and test changes rather than diagnosing people.

Agile implication

Connect concepts to observable team behaviours and experiments.

What you can do

  1. Make the current system visible.
  2. Ask one question that challenges an assumption.
  3. Run a small, reversible experiment.
  4. Inspect evidence and decide what to do next.

Practical example

Choose one recurring friction—handoffs, unclear priorities, excessive WIP or delayed feedback. Map the path from request to outcome, identify where waiting occurs and test one change with the people doing the work.
