Prepare without scripting yourself

A little reflection helps. Too much preparation can make you defend a pre-written answer. Bring enough direction to start, then allow the conversation to move.

60-second preparation

What is taking up the most space in my mind?
What would I like to leave the session with?
Why does this matter now?
What might I be avoiding or protecting?
How open am I to seeing this differently?
Good enough is enough. You can arrive with uncertainty. “I want more clarity” is a valid starting point.
