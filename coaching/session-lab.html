How to use this lab

Don't hunt for the “perfect question.”

Notice your impulseDo you want to solve, reassure, challenge, teach or explore?
Follow the client's meaningListen for words, emotion, assumptions, values and shifts in energy.
Protect agencyUseful coaching expands the client's thinking without quietly choosing for them.
Re-contract when neededIf the conversation moves, check whether the new direction serves the client's desired outcome.
The feedback here is reflective, not an ICF credentialing assessment. Several responses can be useful depending on context, tone and what the client needs.
DEBRIEF

After the scenario

Use these questions to convert practice into deliberate learning.

Where did I lead?

Which response was shaped more by my preferred solution than the client's words?

What did I notice?

What emotion, assumption, value or contradiction could I have explored further?

What will I practise?

Choose one behaviour for your next real session: cleaner questions, more silence, stronger contracting, or lighter observations.
