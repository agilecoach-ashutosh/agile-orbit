Professional Coaching Agreement

Use this checklist to review the business and engagement terms around coaching. It is educational—not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific review.

1. Coaching relationship

Define coaching and clarify what it is not: therapy, legal advice, financial advice, medical care or another specialist service.

2. Scope & logistics

Number of sessions, duration, frequency, delivery channel, messaging access, group elements and included materials.

3. Responsibilities

Coach commitments, client participation, attendance, honesty, between-session work and responsibility for decisions.

4. Confidentiality

What is confidential, applicable limits, data handling, sponsor arrangements and how information is protected.

5. Fees & payment

Full fee, due dates, accepted payment methods, missed payments, cancellation terms and refund policy.

6. Termination

How either party can end the relationship, treatment of unused sessions and what happens after termination.

7. Dispute handling

How disputes will be addressed. Legal language here depends heavily on jurisdiction and should be reviewed appropriately.

8. Ethics commitment

If relevant to the coach's credential/representation, clearly state the professional ethical framework the coach follows.

Also check before signing

  • Are sponsor/client responsibilities clear in organisational coaching?
  • Are recording, transcription and AI use explicitly addressed?
  • Are intellectual-property rights for worksheets/materials clear?
  • Are emergency/referral boundaries and specialist limitations clear?
  • Would a reasonable client understand the terms without needing to decode legal jargon?
