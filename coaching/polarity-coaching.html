Polarity Thinking in Coaching

Some tensions are not problems to solve once. They are pairs that need ongoing attention: autonomy and alignment, stability and change, candour and care.

Problem to solve

Has a durable endpoint. Once resolved, you do not need the opposite option to keep the result healthy.

Polarity to manage

Both poles carry value. Over-focusing on either side creates predictable downsides and eventually increases the need for the other.

Coaching questions

  • Are you trying to choose between two things that may both be necessary?
  • What do you value about each side?
  • What happens when you over-correct toward one side?
  • What would “enough of both” look like this week?
  • What signal would tell you it is time to strengthen the neglected pole?
