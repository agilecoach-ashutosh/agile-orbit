Why start here?

The title captures one of the most useful shifts in coaching: stop treating the presenting problem as an object the coach must fix. Pay attention to how the person is thinking, making meaning and seeing themselves in relation to the situation.

Practice ideas to take from the book

Listen beyond the story

What belief, identity, value or assumption is shaping how the person experiences the problem?

Reflective inquiry

Use observations and questions together to help the client examine their thinking rather than only generating solutions.

Notice your urge to fix

If you are designing the solution faster than the client is thinking, you may be coaching the problem instead of the person.

Let insight change the problem

The external situation may remain the same while the client's relationship to it—and therefore their options—changes significantly.

Before your next session

  • What is the client saying the problem is?
  • Who is the client being in relation to that problem?
  • What are you tempted to solve?
  • What might become visible if you stop chasing the answer?
