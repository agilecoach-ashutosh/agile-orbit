Chemistry Meeting

A chemistry meeting helps coach and potential client understand the coaching relationship and decide whether working together makes sense. The learning map structures the conversation around six topics.

1. What is CoachingClarify what coaching is and how it differs from other forms of support.
2. The Coaching RelationshipSet expectations for how coach and client will work together.
3. ConfidentialityDiscuss how confidentiality will work in the relationship.
4. Logistics & Next StepsCover the practical arrangements and what happens if both decide to proceed.
5. Expectations & BoundariesMake the limits and expectations of the coaching relationship explicit.
6. Final Wrap-upClose the chemistry conversation and leave space for the client's decision.
Scope: this page follows the six-topic chemistry-meeting structure in the supplied coaching learning map. It is intentionally not turned into a sales script. If the engagement proceeds, move next to Coaching Agreements.
