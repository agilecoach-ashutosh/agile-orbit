Your coaching journey

Coaching is rarely a straight line. A useful engagement moves between awareness, experiments, learning and new questions as your context changes.

1. DiscoveryWhat is happening and what matters enough to explore?
2. Overall GoalWhat would meaningful change or learning look like?
3. SessionsExplore what is present now, not just the original plan.
4. Between SessionsNotice patterns, test small experiments and collect learning.
5. Review & CloseWhat has changed, what remains, and what will continue without the coach?

Between sessions matters

Coaching is not only what happens in the conversation. You may notice a trigger differently, try a new behaviour, have a difficult conversation, make a decision or simply observe a pattern without changing it yet.

What am I noticing that I did not notice before?
What experiment did I try, and what did I learn?
What became more complicated—not less?
What feels worth bringing to the next session?

Signs coaching may be working

  • You see more choices than before.
  • You understand your own patterns better.
  • You make decisions with more clarity.
  • You need the coach less, not more.
  • You can carry learning into new situations.
Closure is part of coaching. A healthy coaching relationship includes reviewing whether coaching still adds value and ending intentionally when it no longer needs to continue.
