CONTRACTING CHECK

Before you begin

Does the client know what coaching is?

Clarify what the coach will and will not provide, including the distinction from consulting, mentoring, therapy and other specialist services.

Is confidentiality explicit?

Do not rely on assumptions, especially when an employer, sponsor, HR function or other stakeholder is involved.

Can the agreement change?

Yes. Re-contract when the work shifts, a new stakeholder appears, technology changes, or the client wants something different.
