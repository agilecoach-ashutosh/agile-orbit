One question. Then listen.

Questions are not valuable because they sound powerful. They are useful when they help this client think, notice or choose. Pick an intent—not a clever line.

Outcome
What would make this conversation genuinely useful for you?

Before asking

What did the client just say? What are you curious about because it serves the client—not because you want to display insight?

After asking

Stop. Let the silence work. Do not stack three questions together or rescue the client from thinking time.

If the question misses

Let it miss. The client can redirect. A clean coaching partnership does not require every question to land.
