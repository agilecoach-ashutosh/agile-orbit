BETWEEN SESSIONS

Reflection becomes useful when it changes attention

Observe

Carry one question or pattern into daily life instead of trying to remember the entire coaching conversation.

Experiment

Try a small behaviour that can create new information rather than making a heroic commitment you cannot sustain.

Return

At the next session, begin with what changed, what did not, and what you learned — not just whether you completed a task.
