COACHING STANCES

Choose the right stance

The skill is not staying in one stance. It is making your stance intentional, transparent and useful.

Coach

Use inquiry when the person or team can discover their own answer and ownership matters more than speed.

Facilitator

Design the process when a group needs inclusive participation, shared understanding, a decision or a clear outcome.

Teacher

Explain a concept when a knowledge gap blocks progress. Keep teaching short, contextual and connected to practice.

Mentor

Share experience, options and patterns when someone explicitly wants guidance from what you have learned before.

Consultant

Offer diagnosis or recommendations when expertise is required. Make assumptions visible and keep the client accountable for the decision.

Change Agent

Work across boundaries when the obstacle is systemic: structure, policy, governance, incentives, dependencies or leadership behaviour.

INTERVENTION LOOP

From observation to experiment

A practical loop for coaching without turning improvement into a rollout programme.

01

Observe

What is actually happening? Look at work, conversations, decisions, delays, hand-offs and repeated patterns.

02

Make sense

Separate facts from interpretation. Ask whose perspective is missing and what the system may be rewarding.

03

Contract

Agree what needs attention, what success would look like and what role the coach is being asked to play.

04

Experiment

Choose the smallest meaningful change that can generate evidence quickly rather than designing a perfect future state.

05

Inspect

What changed? What did not? What did we learn? Keep, adapt or stop the experiment based on evidence.

06

Transfer ownership

Build capability so the team or leader can continue without the coach becoming part of the operating model.

Coaching test: a useful intervention should increase the system's ability to notice, decide and improve without creating dependency on the coach.
