AGILE COACHING

Coach the whole system

In Agile organizations a leader is responsible only in one area. Either PEOPLE, PRODUCT, TECHNOLOGY or PROCESS. Agile coaches coach the people in all areas to improve value and flow continuously.

Explore coaching themes ↓See the coaching lens
WHAT IS AGILE COACHING?
The Agile Coaching Institute defines Agile Coaching as “a collaboration with people in a thought provoking and creative journey using coaching approaches with an agile mindset and principles to help individuals, teams and organizations be the best they can be”.
Agile Coaching Institute
AGILE ORBIT COACHING LENS

Four areas. One system.

A visible delivery problem rarely belongs to only one area. Agile Coaching looks across People, Product, Technology and Process to understand what is really limiting value and flow.

VISIBLE SYMPTOM“The team is slow.”

Is knowledge concentrated in one person? Are priorities changing? Is architecture tightly coupled? Is work waiting for approvals? The coach explores the system before diagnosing the symptom.

ONE SYSTEM · FOUR LENSES

Coach the cause, not the label

The same symptom can emerge from very different parts of the system. Explore across the four areas before deciding where to intervene.

SCENARIO 01

“The team is slow.”

PEOPLE One specialist holds critical knowledge.

PRODUCT Priorities keep changing mid-flow.

TECHNOLOGY Services require coordinated releases.

PROCESS Approvals add days of waiting.

SCENARIO 02

“AI made developers faster, but delivery isn't faster.”

PEOPLE Reviewers are overloaded.

PRODUCT More output is created without sharper priorities.

TECHNOLOGY AI accelerates code creation.

PROCESS Review and testing become the new queue.

SCENARIO 03

“Everything is urgent.”

PEOPLE Teams rarely challenge stakeholder requests.

PRODUCT Trade-offs are not explicit.

TECHNOLOGY Fragility makes every release risky.

PROCESS Unlimited WIP normalises constant pressure.

COACHING MINDSET

Make the system visible. Help people improve it.

Don't diagnose the person when the system is creating the behaviour.

Don't optimise activity when value is unclear.

Don't accelerate one part of delivery while moving the queue somewhere else.

Build the capability to improve without creating dependence on the coach.
