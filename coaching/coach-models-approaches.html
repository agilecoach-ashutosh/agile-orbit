Models & Tools

Use frameworks as scaffolding, not scripts. A model or tool earns its place only when it helps the client notice, think or choose more clearly.

INTERACTIVE TOOLS

Make thinking visible

A visual tool should create awareness, not replace the coaching conversation or calculate the “right” answer.

Interactive🎡

Wheel of Life

Compare current and desired satisfaction across important life areas and use the gaps as prompts for reflection.

Perspective

Look at one situation through different lenses to loosen assumptions and widen possible choices.
