Progress Ladder

Use a 1–10 scale to reveal what is already working, what the next small movement would look like, and what “enough for now” means to the client.

Scale the topic
5
1 · very little10 · fully where I want to be
7
Set a current position and an “enough for now” position. The aim is movement, not perfection.
Coach stance

Do not use the scale as a scorecard.

Ask why it is not lower.This surfaces resources, progress and capability already present.
Define the next number behaviourally.What would the client actually be doing differently?
Avoid worshipping 10.The useful destination may be 7, not perfection.
