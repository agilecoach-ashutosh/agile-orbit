Coaching Psychology Foundations

The learning map distinguishes a theory from a model: a theory helps explain the terrain; a model helps navigate it in practice. These are the psychology foundations named in the coaching learning map.

Theory

Humanistic Psychology

People have an innate capacity for growth, self-awareness and self-direction.

Theory

Cognitive Behavioural

Thoughts influence feelings and actions. Reframing limiting beliefs can lead to different outcomes.

Theory

Adult Learning / Andragogy

Self-learning, experience-based learning, goal orientation, relevance, problem-centred learning and internal motivation are highlighted in the learning map.

Theory

Solution Focused

Emphasises solutions rather than problems. It assumes clients already possess resources, strengths and knowledge that can support positive change; coaching helps unlock and amplify these.

Theory

Transformational Learning

The learning map identifies Transformational Learning as a coaching psychology foundation. The supplied material names it but does not elaborate it further here.

Theory

Positive Psychology

Focuses on the scientific study of human strengths, well-being and optimal functioning. The learning map specifically references Martin Seligman's PERMA model.

Theory

Systems Theory

The characteristics of parts cannot be fully explained in isolation from the whole; interactions and context matter, producing systems that can be complex and unpredictable.

Keep the distinction: these foundations are lenses for understanding coaching, not scripts for running a session. Existing practical models such as GROW, CLEAR and OSKAR remain under Models & Approaches.
