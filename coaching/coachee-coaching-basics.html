What is coaching?

A coaching conversation gives you space to think more deeply, notice patterns and make choices you own. The coach brings presence, curiosity and process—not the answer to your life.

What happens in coaching?

You bring what matters. Together, you clarify what would make the conversation useful, explore what is happening beneath the surface, notice new perspectives and decide what you want to do with the awareness.

ArriveWhat is present?
AgreeWhat would be useful?
ExploreWhat is really happening?
DiscoverWhat are you seeing now?
ChooseWhat do you want next?

You do not need to...

  • arrive with a perfectly formed goal;
  • know the answer before the conversation starts;
  • take action just because an insight appears;
  • agree with the coach's observations;
  • continue coaching if it is no longer useful.

Coaching vs other support

Coaching: helps you think and choose.

Mentoring: draws on someone's experience and guidance.

Consulting: brings specialist recommendations.

Teaching: develops knowledge or skill.

Therapy: addresses needs requiring a qualified mental-health professional.

A useful test: if you mainly need somebody to tell you the correct answer, coaching may not be the only—or best—form of support for that moment.
