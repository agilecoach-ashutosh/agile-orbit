Goal → Obstacle → Plan

Move from aspiration to a realistic response. Name the desired outcome, surface the inner obstacle, then build an if–then plan the client actually owns.

1GoalWhat do I want to move toward?
2OutcomeWhat would be different if this moved?
3ObstacleWhat in me could get in the way?
4PlanIf the obstacle appears, what will I do?
Build the chain from goal → desired outcome → inner obstacle → if–then response.
Coach stance

Keep the obstacle close to the client's influence.

External reality matters.Do not turn every structural constraint into a mindset problem.
Look for the inner friction.Habit, avoidance, perfectionism, fear, interpretation, or competing values may be relevant if the client names them.
Make the plan small.A useful plan is observable and repeatable, not heroic.
