What can I bring to coaching?

Almost anything that benefits from reflection, awareness, choice or growth. Start with the situation that has the most energy—not the topic that sounds most impressive.

🧭

Career & Direction

“I am doing well, but I do not know what I want next.”

Explore: What matters most about the next chapter?
👔

Leadership

“I want to lead better without controlling everything.”

Explore: What kind of leader do I want to be here?
⚖️

Decision & Dilemma

“I keep going back and forth between two options.”

Explore: What is this decision really asking of me?
💪

Confidence

“I know I can do this, but something keeps holding me back.”

Explore: What does confidence mean in this situation?
🗣️

Difficult Conversations

“There is a conversation I keep avoiding.”

Explore: What makes this conversation hard?
🤝

Relationships

“Why does working with this person affect me so much?”

Explore: What pattern am I part of?
🎯

Goals & Accountability

“I know what I want to do, but I keep not doing it.”

Explore: What keeps winning when this goal loses?
🧠

Beliefs & Assumptions

“Maybe I am looking at this in only one way.”

Explore: What am I treating as true?
🧭

Purpose & Values

“What actually matters to me now?”

Explore: Which values need a stronger voice?
🔀

Change & Transition

“Everything is changing and I do not know how I want to respond.”

Explore: What do I want to carry forward?
⚡

Boundaries & Priorities

“I say yes to everything and then feel overwhelmed.”

Explore: What does saying yes protect?
❓

I Don't Know Yet

“Something feels off, but I cannot explain what.”

Start with: What keeps returning to my mind?
You do not need a polished topic. “I feel stuck,” “I keep thinking about this,” or “I do not know what I want” can be enough to begin.
