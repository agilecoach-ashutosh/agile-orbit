Dialogic Orientation Quadrant (DOQ)

Haesun Moon's DOQ is a heuristic for making the interactional nature of coaching dialogue visible—helping coaches notice how their language participates in shaping meaning, purpose, possibilities and progress.

Why this belongs in coaching practice

A coach does not merely ask neutral questions. Every word, sequence, emphasis and response can make some parts of the client's experience more available and others less available. DOQ gives coaches a way to observe those conversational choices more critically.

Notice the interaction

Listen not only to what the client says, but to what your response invites next.

Track movement

What language seems to expand purpose, possibility or evidence of progress?

Review transcripts

Use the framework after a session to study your conversational orientation instead of relying only on memory.

Refine—not perform

The aim is not to sound “coach-like.” It is to become more deliberate about what your words do in interaction.

Agile Orbit practice lens

Take one client sentence and ask:

  • What did I choose to respond to?
  • What did my response make more discussable?
  • What possibility did my language open—or close?
  • Did I move toward the client's purpose or toward my preferred interpretation?
  • What alternative response might have produced a different conversational direction?
