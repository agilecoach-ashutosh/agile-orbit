AI-augmented coaching

Keep the client’s thinking and choice central when considering AI support.

Agree the boundary first

Discuss whether AI has a useful role, what information it would receive and how its output would be checked. Do not enter identifying client material without an explicit agreement and an appropriate data-handling arrangement.

Use AI for preparation and reflection

Work with fictional examples to explore possible questions, organise a learning plan or rehearse a conversation. Treat generated interpretations as hypotheses, not facts about a client.

Keep responsibility with people

The coach remains responsible for professional judgment. The client chooses their meaning and next steps. AI suggestions do not replace consent, supervision or appropriate specialist support.
