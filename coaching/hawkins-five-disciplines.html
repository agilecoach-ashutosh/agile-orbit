Hawkins Five Disciplines

A systemic lens developed by Peter Hawkins for looking at what effective teams need to attend to beyond internal team dynamics.

CommissioningWhat does the wider system need from this team?
ClarifyingWhat is the team's shared purpose, mandate and success?
Co-CreatingHow does the team work together internally?
ConnectingHow does the team engage stakeholders and the wider system?
Core LearningHow does the team reflect, learn and renew itself?

Use it as a diagnostic conversation—not a scorecard

Commissioning

Whose expectations shape the team's work? Are there conflicting demands from sponsors, customers or other stakeholders?

Clarifying

Can the team explain why it exists, what value it creates and what “good” looks like?

Co-Creating

What interaction patterns, norms, roles and decision habits help or hinder collective performance?

Connecting

How well does the team build relationships across boundaries and adapt to stakeholder feedback?

Core Learning

Does the team pause to learn from experience, challenge assumptions and improve how it works?

Team coaching prompt

If the team spent the next quarter improving only one discipline, which one would create the most useful ripple effect—and what evidence supports that choice?

Framework attributed to Professor Peter Hawkins / School of Systemic Team Coaching. Agile Orbit presents an original summary and coaching prompts. Background article: The five disciplines of highly effective teams ↗
