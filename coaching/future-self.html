Future Self Explorer

Step into a meaningful future perspective, notice what changed, and bring one realistic behaviour back to today. The aim is possibility—not prediction.

Choose a horizon
Imagine a meaningful future—not a perfect one—and then bring one behaviour back to today.
Coach stance

Do not turn this into fantasy or forecasting.

Make it meaningful, not perfect.The future can still contain uncertainty, trade-offs and challenge.
Listen for identity shifts.What is the client doing, believing or allowing differently?
Bridge back to today.The exercise becomes useful when one future behaviour can be tested now.
