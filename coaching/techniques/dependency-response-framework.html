Dependency Response Framework

Classify a dependency response as Control, Influence, Escalate or Accept / Manage.

PROCESSDEPENDENCY & CONSTRAINT COACHINGAGILE ORBIT ORIGINAL
← Back to Dependency & Constraint CoachingUnderstand the method ↓
Core idea: Help teams act where they have agency while making system-level constraints visible to the people who can change them.
CONTROL→INFLUENCE→ESCALATE→ACCEPT / MANAGE

Understand the method first

Control

The team can remove or reduce the dependency directly—for example through skill building, automation or changing its own workflow.

Influence

Another team or stakeholder is needed, but collaboration, interfaces, timing or working agreements can improve the dependency.

Escalate

The constraint sits beyond team authority and needs leadership, policy, funding or organisational redesign.

Accept / Manage

The dependency is legitimate for now. Make it predictable, engage early and reduce its frequency or impact.

Lineage: Agile Orbit synthesis created for Dependency & Constraint Coaching. It is intentionally labelled as an Agile Orbit framework rather than an external industry standard.

How to use it

  1. Name what the team is waiting for and why the dependency exists.
  2. Collect evidence about frequency, wait time and impact on value/flow.
  3. Choose the response path based on actual authority and influence.
  4. Define one action, owner and review date.
  5. Reclassify when the system changes; today’s accepted constraint may become tomorrow’s removable dependency.

Coach with it

Use the method as a lens for inquiry, not as a rigid prescription. Useful coaching questions include:

“Can the team change this without external permission?”

“If not, can the relationship or interface be improved?”

“What evidence would make leadership act on this system constraint?”

“If we must live with it, how do we stop being surprised by it?”

Agile example

A specialist-knowledge dependency is controlled through pairing; a shared-platform coordination issue is influenced through joint refinement; a mandatory architecture approval is escalated with blocked-time evidence; regulatory sign-off is accepted and engaged earlier.

Watch for

Watch for

Do not use “Accept” as a polite synonym for ignoring the problem.

Watch for

Escalate with evidence and a clear request, not only frustration.
