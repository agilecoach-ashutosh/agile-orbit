Professional Coaching

One conversation. Two very different perspectives.

Professional coaching can be experienced from either side of the relationship. Come here to make better use of coaching as a coachee, or to deepen how you think, listen, contract and practise as a coach.

THE TWO SIDES CONNECT

Good coaching becomes clearer when you understand both experiences.

A coach benefits from remembering what it feels like to be the person doing the thinking. A coachee benefits from understanding that the coach is not there to own the answer. Explore the other path whenever that perspective would help.
