Frameworks that support—not control—the conversation

Use a model as scaffolding when it helps you orient. If you are thinking harder about the next letter in the acronym than listening to the client, let the model go.

Goal-focused

GROW

Goal → Reality → Options → Will / Way Forward

Useful when the client wants movement toward a defined goal.

  • What would you like to achieve?
  • What is happening now?
  • What possibilities are available?
  • What do you want to do next?
Topic emerging

TGROW

Topic → Goal → Reality → Options → Will

Useful when the client arrives with a broad or fuzzy concern.

  • What feels worth exploring today?
  • What would make this conversation useful?
  • What is true now?
  • What do you want to take forward?
Developmental

CLEAR

Contract → Listen → Explore → Action → Review

Useful for deeper developmental conversations where contracting and review matter.

  • What do we need to agree?
  • What are you hearing beneath the content?
  • What is underneath the obvious issue?
  • What changed or was learned?
Solution-focused

OSKAR

Outcome → Scaling → Know-how → Affirm & Action → Review

Useful when the client benefits from noticing resources and visible movement.

  • What would better look like?
  • Where are you now from 1–10?
  • What is already helping?
  • What small move would shift the number?
Possibility

Solution-Focused Coaching

Preferred future → Exceptions → Resources → Small steps

Useful when problem analysis has become repetitive and the client wants to notice what already works.

  • If this were better, what would you notice first?
  • When is the problem less powerful?
  • What are you already doing that helps?
Strengths

Appreciative Inquiry

Discover → Dream → Design → Destiny / Deliver

Useful for strengths-based change and possibility thinking.

  • When has this worked at its best?
  • What would an ideal future look like?
  • What conditions would make that possible?
  • What will sustain movement?
Coach self-check: Am I using the model to support the client's thinking, or am I making the client's thinking fit my model?
