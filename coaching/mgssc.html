Marshall Goldsmith Stakeholder Centered Coaching

The session deck presents MGSSC as a stakeholder-centred change cycle focused on involving stakeholders, feedforward, behaviour change and changed perceptions.

1. AskBegin with stakeholder input.
2. ListenHear the stakeholder perspective.
3. ThankAcknowledge the input.
4. ThinkReflect on what was heard.
5. RespondUse feedforward and respond constructively.
6. ChangeWork on observable behaviour change.
7. Follow UpReturn to stakeholders and continue the cycle.

Involve Stakeholders

The model places the leader's stakeholders at the centre of the development process rather than relying only on coach–client dialogue.

Emphasize Feedforward

The learning material highlights feedforward as part of the cycle, supporting attention to future behaviour.

Change Behaviour & Perceptions

The cycle links behaviour change with how that change is experienced and perceived by stakeholders over time.
