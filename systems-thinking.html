What it means

Look at relationships, feedback loops, constraints and unintended consequences.

What to look for

  • Observable behaviours and repeated patterns.
  • Context, incentives and constraints.
  • How people respond to uncertainty and feedback.
  • Where the system makes a desired behaviour easier or harder.
