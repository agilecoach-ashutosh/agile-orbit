Sprint Scope Reality Check

Challenge a proposed Sprint scope by surfacing risk, uncertainty, assumptions and dependencies.

Scrum Master / Agile Delivery CoachSprint PlanningAnalyze + Challenge
PURPOSE

Why use this prompt?

Instead of asking AI whether the team can complete the stories, use it to identify risk and assumptions. The team can then use those signals to have a better Sprint Planning conversation.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Prompt

AGILE ORBIT / PROMPT
Prompt:
Act as a skeptical Agile Delivery Coach.

Review the proposed Sprint scope below:

[SPRINT BACKLOG]

Team capacity:
[CAPACITY]

Historical average velocity:
[VELOCITY]

Identify:
- Potential overcommitment
- Large or risky stories
- Dependencies
- Unclear requirements
- Stories with high uncertainty
- Technical spikes that may be required
- External dependencies
- Testing/integration risks

Do not decide whether the team should commit.

Instead, provide 5-7 questions the Development Team should discuss before finalizing the Sprint scope.

Copy the prompt as-is, then replace the input placeholders with your own context.

✦
Agile Principle

AI can challenge the plan. The team owns the commitment. Use the output to improve the conversation, not to outsource the commitment decision.
