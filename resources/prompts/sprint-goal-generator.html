Sprint Goal Generator

Turn a set of selected stories into outcome-focused Sprint Goal options.

Product Owner / Scrum MasterSprint PlanningSynthesize + Generate
PURPOSE

Why use this prompt?

Give AI the selected stories and ask it to identify the common business outcome or customer value. The prompt produces three Sprint Goal options and explains the purpose behind each one.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Prompt

AGILE ORBIT / PROMPT
Act as an experienced Scrum Product Owner.

We are planning the next Sprint.

Here are the stories selected for consideration:

[PASTE STORIES]

Identify the common business outcome or customer value that connects these stories.

Generate 3 possible Sprint Goals:
1. Outcome-focused
2. Customer-focused
3. Business-value-focused

Do not simply combine the story titles.

A Sprint Goal should describe the valuable outcome the team intends to achieve, not a list of activities.

For each option, explain why it represents the Sprint's purpose.

Copy the prompt as-is, then replace the input placeholders with your own context.

✦
Facilitation Tip

Ask participants: “Which Sprint Goal would give the team the most flexibility if some stories change during the Sprint?” This creates a useful discussion about Sprint Goal versus Sprint Scope.
