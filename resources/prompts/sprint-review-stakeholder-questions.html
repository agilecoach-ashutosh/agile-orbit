Sprint Review Stakeholder Question Generator

Prepare for realistic stakeholder questions before a Sprint Review.

PURPOSE

Why use this prompt?

Help less experienced Product Owners and teams anticipate questions stakeholders might reasonably ask about the Sprint outcome, including value, business impact, usability, risks, adoption and evidence.

COPY-READY PROMPT

Prompt

AGILE ORBIT / PROMPT
Act as a skeptical but constructive stakeholder.

Based on the following Sprint Review content:

[CONTENT]

Generate 10 questions stakeholders might reasonably ask.

Include questions about:
- Customer value
- Business impact
- Usability
- Risks
- Performance
- Security
- Adoption
- Missing functionality
- Future opportunities
- Evidence/data

Copy the prompt as-is, then replace the input placeholders with your own context.

✦
Why it helps

This helps teams prepare for real stakeholder conversations while keeping the questions grounded in the Sprint Review content.
