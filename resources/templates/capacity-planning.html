How to use it

  1. Set the Sprint Start Date and Sprint End Date.
  2. Enter each team member's allocated days and expected deductions.
  3. Review Actual Working Days and the team capacity summary.
  4. Enter the last three Sprint velocities under N-2, N-1 and N.
  5. Use the calculated Predictable Velocity as a planning input — not as a commitment.
Agile Orbit principle: Capacity is an input to Sprint planning, not a promise of delivery. Use the numbers to support a team conversation about realistic scope.
