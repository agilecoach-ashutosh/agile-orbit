AI · LEARNING PATH

Build AI fluency in four levels.

You do not need to become an AI engineer to work effectively with AI. Progress from using AI well, to extending it, to building workflows, and finally to redesigning how work happens.

LEVEL 1USE AI

Prompt, provide context, verify

Learn how to frame a task, provide useful context, constrain the output, ask for structured responses and validate important claims.

Prompt LibraryResponsible AI
LEVEL 2EXTEND AI

Skills, plugins and connectors

Move beyond one-off prompts. Add reusable know-how with skills, then understand how connectors and MCP expose approved tools and context.

AI Skills & PluginsMCP & Connectors
LEVEL 3BUILD WITH AI

Agents and workflows

Combine goals, context, skills, tools, state and guardrails into multi-step workflows. Decide where AI can act and where people must approve.

AI AgentsAgent workflow builder
LEVEL 4TRANSFORM WORK

Human + AI operating model

Redesign roles, decisions, governance, measures and workflows so AI reduces low-value effort without weakening accountability, collaboration or professional judgment.

AI ToolsGovernance guardrails
LEARNING PRINCIPLE

Do not measure AI maturity by the number of tools you use.

Measure whether work becomes clearer, faster, safer or more valuable—and whether teams understand when not to delegate judgment to AI.
