SPOTLIGHT · NIST AI RMF

GOVERN → MAP → MEASURE → MANAGE

NIST AI RMF is a voluntary, cross-sector framework for managing risks to people, organizations and society across the AI lifecycle. Its four functions create a practical operating rhythm rather than a one-time checklist.

GOVERNPolicies, roles, accountability, culture
MAPContext, stakeholders, impacts, risks
MEASUREEvaluate, test, monitor, document
MANAGEPrioritize, respond, monitor, improve

Current-status cue: AI RMF 1.0 remains the published framework while NIST is revising it. The NIST Generative AI Profile is a companion resource for GenAI-specific risks.

Translate it into product delivery

GOVERNDefine / Plan — ownership, policy, decision rights and guardrails.MAPDiscover / Define — users, context, harms, assumptions and affected stakeholders.MEASURETest / Measure — evaluations, red teaming, metrics, evidence and monitoring.MANAGERelease / Improve — prioritize risk treatment, operate controls, respond and learn.

DON'T CONFUSE THEM

Framework vs standard vs regulation vs security guide

ExampleWhat it isPrimary questionUse it when…
NIST AI RMFRisk frameworkWhat AI risks should we identify, measure and manage?You need an organization-wide risk operating model.
ISO/IEC 42001Management-system standardHow should the organization govern AI systematically?You want repeatable policies, processes, oversight and continual improvement.
EU AI ActRegulationWhat legal obligations apply to this AI use?Your provider/deployer activities fall within EU regulatory scope.
OWASP GenAI / Agentic Top 10Security guidanceWhat application and agent vulnerabilities should we defend against?You are designing, testing or reviewing GenAI/agent systems.
MITRE ATLASThreat knowledge baseHow might adversaries attack or abuse AI systems?You are threat modelling, red teaming or investigating AI attacks.
OECD AI PrinciplesPrinciplesWhat should trustworthy, human-centred AI aim for?You need values and policy principles that guide decisions.
