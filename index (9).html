Agile Coaching

A practical guide to coaching, facilitation, mentoring and teaching.

Key takeaway: Agile coaching is less about having the answer and more about creating conditions for people to learn and act.

Concept

Agile coaching is less about having the answer and more about creating conditions for people to learn and act.

What happens in teams

Choose a stance deliberately: teach when knowledge is missing, mentor when experience can be shared, facilitate when the group needs a process, and coach when the person can discover their own path.

Agile implication

Keep the client system—not the coach—at the centre.

What you can do

  1. Make the current system visible.
  2. Ask one question that challenges an assumption.
  3. Run a small, reversible experiment.
  4. Inspect evidence and decide what to do next.

Practical example

Choose one recurring friction—handoffs, unclear priorities, excessive WIP or delayed feedback. Map the path from request to outcome, identify where waiting occurs and test one change with the people doing the work.
