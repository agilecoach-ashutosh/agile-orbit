AGILE ORBIT

Interview Questions

Expandable preparation for Scrum Master, Agile Coach and leadership conversations.

Explore the system before prescribing a solution: goal clarity, slicing, dependencies, interruptions, capacity, quality and stakeholder change. Then agree one experiment and inspect the result.

Start from outcomes. Combine delivery, quality, flow and outcome signals rather than using velocity as a productivity score.

Understand the concern, make the change hypothesis explicit, involve the people affected and create a safe experiment where evidence can shape the next step.
