HOW TO USE IT

Make the trade-off visible.

Meeting cost does not tell you whether a meeting is valuable. It helps make the investment visible so you can ask whether the purpose, participants and cadence justify that investment.

Direct people-cost only. This does not include preparation time, context switching, travel, tooling or opportunity cost.
