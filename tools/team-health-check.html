TEAM REFLECTION TOOL

Team Health Check

Reflect across eight dimensions of team health and surface the signals worth discussing together.

Rate each area from 1 (needs attention) to 5 (strong). This is a reflection tool, not a scientific diagnostic. The pattern matters more than a single overall number.

HOW TO USE IT

Discuss the pattern, not the score.

A healthy-looking average can hide a serious weakness in one dimension. Use the lowest-scoring areas to start a conversation and compare perspectives across team members.

Best used as a conversation starter. If possible, let people rate independently first, then discuss where perceptions differ and choose one small experiment.
