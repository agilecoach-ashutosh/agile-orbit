WSJF Calculator

Compare features using Weighted Shortest Job First: Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size.

HOW IT WORKS

Make the economic trade-off visible.

WSJF = Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size

WSJF helps compare the relative economic value of delivering work sooner against the size required. Use one consistent relative scoring scale across all features.

Use the modified Fibonacci-style relative scale [1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20].
Use the same scale for Business Value, Time Criticality, Risk Reduction / Opportunity Enablement, and Job Size. Scores are relative to the other features being assessed—not absolute measurements.
If two features have the same WSJF: first check whether the relative scores still make sense. If the tie is genuine, use secondary considerations such as dependencies, sequencing, strategic fit, learning value or risk instead of forcing an artificial difference.

WSJF

Interpretation
Higher WSJF indicates work that may deserve earlier consideration.

SCORING GUIDANCE

Powerful questions before you score

Discuss the questions comparatively across features. The goal is shared understanding—not false precision.

📈User / Business Value
  • Which customer or business outcome improves if we deliver this?
  • What revenue, cost, adoption or customer-satisfaction impact could this create?
  • What negative consequence or penalty occurs if we delay or never do it?
Think relative value compared with the other items being scored.
⏱️Time Criticality
  • Is there a fixed date, regulatory window, market event or opportunity deadline?
  • How quickly does the value decay if we wait?
  • What becomes more costly, risky or less useful if delivery is delayed?
High time criticality means the economic impact of waiting rises quickly.
🛡️Risk Reduction / Opportunity Enablement
  • Which current or future risk does this reduce?
  • What learning, information or uncertainty reduction will this create?
  • What future capability, dependency or business opportunity does this unlock?
Include enablers whose value comes from reducing uncertainty or enabling later work.
⚙️Job Size
  • How much total effort is required end-to-end relative to the other features?
  • Where are the major complexity, uncertainty or dependency drivers?
  • Do testing, integration, compliance, migration or rollout materially increase the size?
Estimate the whole job, not only development effort.
Facilitation tip: score one reference feature first, then compare the others against it. If the team debates whether something is a 5 or an 8, the conversation about assumptions is often more valuable than the number itself.
