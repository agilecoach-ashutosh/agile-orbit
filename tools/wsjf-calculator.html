WSJF Calculator

Compare features using Weighted Shortest Job First: Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size.

HOW IT WORKS

Make the economic trade-off visible.

WSJF = Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size

WSJF helps compare the relative economic value of delivering work sooner against the effort or size required. Use the same scoring scale across all features.

Cost of Delay
Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction / Opportunity Enablement
WSJF
Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size
Interpretation
Higher WSJF indicates work that may deserve earlier consideration.
