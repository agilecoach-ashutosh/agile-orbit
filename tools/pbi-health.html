PBI Health Calculator

Check whether the top of your Product Backlog has enough refined, ready work to support upcoming Sprint Planning without creating excessive upfront inventory.

HOW IT WORKS

Compare ready work with recent delivery pace.

Health ratio = Ready story points ÷ Average Sprint velocity

This tool uses a practical 2–3× velocity runway as the healthy zone: enough ready work for roughly two to three Sprints, without refining too far into an uncertain future.

Under 2×
The team may run short of sufficiently refined work for upcoming Sprint Planning.
2× to 3×
A practical runway that balances readiness with flexibility.
Over 3×
Too much work may be refined too early, increasing rework and stale understanding.
