☁️

Yesterday's Weather, Tomorrow's Forecast

Weather forecasting starts with what has already been observed and combines it with the current conditions to create a view of what may happen next. It is useful for decisions, but it is never a guarantee — forecasts carry uncertainty.

Agile works in a similar way. Completed Sprint velocity gives us historical evidence. Current team availability gives us today's conditions. Together, they help us create a more realistic forecast for the next Sprint.

🌦️ A forecast informs a decision. It doesn't guarantee the future.

READ THE NUMBER CORRECTLY

A forecast is not a commitment

📈

Use history as evidence

The last three completed Sprint velocities provide a simple historical baseline. The calculator deliberately uses their average rather than assuming the latest Sprint will repeat.

👥

Adjust for today's capacity

Available person-days are converted into an equivalent available headcount and compared with the allocated team capacity.

🧭

Let the team decide

Dependencies, complexity, unplanned work and uncertainty still matter. Use the prediction to support a Sprint conversation, not to replace team judgement.

Agile Orbit principle: Capacity is an input to Sprint planning, not a promise of delivery.
