PI Planning Capacity Calculator

Turn PI calendar time and team availability into a practical capacity view for multiple ART teams.

HOW IT WORKS

Capacity before commitment.

Person-days = People × Available days × Focus factor

Add each ART team, account for non-working time, then use the result as an input to PI Planning—not as a promise of delivery.

1 · Calendar
PI weeks × working days gives the starting workday pool.
2 · Availability
Subtract holidays and leave to find available person-days.
3 · Focus
Apply a realistic focus factor for ceremonies, support, collaboration and other work.
