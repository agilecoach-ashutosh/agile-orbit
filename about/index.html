ABOUT ME

Agile coaching beyond frameworks.

I'm Ashutosh Mishra, an Agile Coach and technology leader passionate about helping people, teams, and organisations navigate complexity, improve continuously, and create meaningful outcomes.

I’m known for building high-performing teams, fostering psychological safety, and embedding an Agile mindset through ICF-aligned coaching.

My journey across Agile, product development, leadership, coaching, and organisational change has reinforced a simple belief: Agile is much more than frameworks, processes, or events. It is about how we think, how we collaborate, how we learn, and how we continuously improve.

I also bring a Lean mindset and behavioural-psychology perspective to coaching—observing team behavioural patterns, identifying the habits and interactions that influence performance, and helping teams consciously improve the behaviours that enable better collaboration, flow, and outcomes.

WHY AGILE ORBIT?
“Make practical Agile knowledge easier to discover, understand, practice, and apply.”

I created Agile Orbit as an open space to bring together the knowledge, tools, practices, and ideas that have helped me throughout my Agile journey.

Whether you're a Scrum Master, Agile Coach, Product Owner, leader, practitioner, or someone beginning your Agile journey, Agile Orbit is designed to help you learn something useful, challenge your thinking, and take something practical back to your team.
