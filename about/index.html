HOW I WORK

Four principles that shape my approach.

01

Ask before prescribing

Understand the problem, context and constraints before introducing a framework or solution.

02

Outcomes over activity

A busy system is not necessarily an effective system. Focus on what changes for customers, teams and the organisation.

03

Evidence over assumptions

Use observation, feedback and meaningful data to challenge intuition and update decisions.

04

Adapt over copy-paste

Frameworks provide useful constraints and language; context determines how the practice should actually be applied.
